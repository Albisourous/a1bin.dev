# snowboard.game — tiny wings, ascii edition

this file runs instead of rendering. open it in the editor.

hold SPACE — or press the snow — to drop into a downhill and build
speed. release to float. land on the downslopes, launch off the crests.
ride until you wipe out — restart starts a new run.

the sun sets as you go. the ringed dots buy it back — one dot, one
second, never more than fifteen banked. miss fifteen and you're done.
the hills never end — stacked sines all the way down.
your best runs land in the board file.
the rest of the site is in the readme.
